Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Shorts Bot is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. It requests the YouTube upload permission (youtube.upload) for the owner's own Google account only.

Shorts Bot does not collect, share, sell, or store personal data from any other users. The OAuth refresh token is stored only as a secret in the owner's GitHub repository settings and is used solely to upload videos to the owner's channel. Google user data is not transferred to any third party.
